For each topic, I will:

Watch a relevant tutorial.

Write down the important concepts and terminology.

Apply the concept to SocialPulse.

Test the implementation.

Document any challenges, decisions, and solutions.

Commit meaningful progress to Git.

The purpose of these resources is to refresh my memory, strengthen my understanding of key concepts, and guide me as I independently implement each part of the SocialPulse project.
